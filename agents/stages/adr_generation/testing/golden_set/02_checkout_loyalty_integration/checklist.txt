clarifications.json — this is the "everything resolved cleanly" case, so the bar here is
completeness, not honest omission. Items marked [DETERMINISTIC] are yes/no, mechanically
checkable with no judgment call. Items marked [JUDGMENT] require reading intent/nuance.

1. [DETERMINISTIC] Section 4 (Affected Components) contains at least these two rows, each
   tagged with exactly this status:
   - CheckoutService — MODIFIED
   - LoyaltyService — NEW

7. [DETERMINISTIC] No component or data contract appears that isn't one of CheckoutService,

   these clarifications establish.
