The system evolved from a Legacy Order Monolith to a gradual migration where Order Service handled new order flows and published Order Events over Kafka, followed by independent Payment, Inventory, and Notification services. Payment processing moved to the Payment Confirmed contract, while Inventory Service published Stock Updated events for Notification Service. In the latest version, the Legacy Order Monolith and Order Events contract are fully retired; the active architecture consists exclusively of Order Service, Payment Service, Inventory Service, and Notification Service.
